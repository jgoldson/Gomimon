MEET YOUR NEW LITTLE COMPANION

A few clicks.
A new friend.

GomiMon is currently available as an unpacked Chrome extension.

  1. Download the extension

    Save the package and unzip it somewhere you’ll keep it.

    Download GomiMon ↓

  2. Open Chrome’s extensions page

    Paste chrome://extensions into your address bar and switch on Developer mode.

  3. Load your new companion

    Click Load unpacked, choose the unzipped folder, then pin GomiMon to your toolbar. Open it to hatch your pet.

Optional AI checks require Google sign-in. You can raise and manually feed your pet without them.

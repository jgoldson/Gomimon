## Detailed description

Meet GomiMon, a little virtual pet that grows as you clean up your Reddit and X feeds.

Hatch your companion, give it a name, and choose its diet. Feed posts manually, hide clearly marked ads, or sign in with Google to use experimental AI checks and topic filters.

• Choose which types of posts to hide using the filters in Settings.
• Watch your pet grow: evolve from Baby-Gomi to Bubble-Gomi at 100 meals and Nimbus-Gomi at 1,000.
• Keep it happy: balance hunger and glitch, and see your latest meals in local Slop history.
• Stay in control: choose Reddit, X, or both; change your filters and restore automatically filtered X posts with Show post.
• Join the meal leaderboard if you want: participation is optional, and public standings show your pet name, evolution, and meal totals.

Platform support
Reddit supports posts and comments. X support covers the For You and Following home feeds. Images and videos are not analyzed. AI checks require at least 30 authored words on Reddit or 10 on X; quoted text does not count toward this minimum.

About AI checks and privacy
AI labels are experimental estimates and can be wrong. GomiMon does not prove who wrote a post, and it does not post, report, or flag content on your behalf. Changes affect only your own browser.

Google sign-in is required for AI checks and semantic topic filters. Text selected for assessment is sent through the GomiMon service to TypeSafe. Clearly marked ads are detected locally. Pet progress and recent Slop history stay in your browser. You can sign out or delete your GomiMon account in Settings.

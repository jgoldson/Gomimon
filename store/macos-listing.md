## Description

Less ragebait. More room for what you love. A little monster to eat the rest.

GomiMon is a Safari extension that cleans up your Reddit and X feeds while you browse. Choose the content you want to see less of, turn on automatic filtering, and let your virtual pet turn unwanted posts into its next meal.

CHOOSE WHAT LEAVES YOUR FEED
Give your GomiMon a diet that suits you. Automatically hide matching posts in the categories you select:
• Ragebait — inflammatory posts built to provoke a reaction.
• AI content — text estimated to be AI-written.
• Ads — clearly marked promoted posts.
• Promotions — promotional and sales-focused content.
• Politics, celebrity gossip, sports, and crypto — topics you can skip whenever you want a break.

You pick the categories. GomiMon helps clear the clutter so there's more room for the conversations, communities, and interests you came for.

TURN SLOP INTO SOMETHING GOOD
Hatch your pet, give it a name, and watch it grow as it eats unwanted posts. Feeding animations make clearing your feed satisfying, from a quick snack to a candlelit fine-dining experience. More meals unlock new evolutions. Check your meal history or join the optional leaderboard to see how your companion stacks up.

AUTOMATIC WHEN YOU WANT IT. HANDS-ON WHEN YOU DON'T.
Let your selected filters work as you scroll, or use Feed to remove an individual post yourself. Adjust filtering strength, change your pet's diet, and choose Reddit, X, or both. Your choices shape your feed.

MADE FOR SAFARI ON MAC
GomiMon works on Reddit and the For You and Following home feeds on X. Filtering changes your own view of the page; it doesn't post, report, or flag content on your behalf.

GET STARTED
Open GomiMon, enable it in Safari Settings → Extensions, and allow access to your chosen sites. Hatch your pet and choose its diet.

FREE TO START CLEANING UP
This Safari version is free, with a daily allowance of remote checks and no purchases or subscriptions. Manual feeding, your local pet, and marked-ad filtering work without an account. AI and topic checks require Apple or Google sign-in and your permission to send selected text to GomiMon and TypeSafe. Pet progress and recent meal history stay on your Mac.

AI and topic assessments are estimates and can be wrong. Checks analyze eligible text, not images or video. Requires macOS 14 or later and Safari.
